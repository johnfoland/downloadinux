# Chunk P0001-C06 — Setup over the CLI, Stage 1 docs, and the migration proof

**Source:** project [P0001](../projects/P0001-portable-config.md), delivery
item D5 (all of it, with its *Done when* as amended 2026-10-07), plus
findings 1, 3, 4, and 5 of the P0001-C05 review. Authority:
[target design](../target-design.md) *Migration* ("Pinned for P0001-C06",
and "Pinned for P0001-C02" for the import step), *Health checks* (the skip
rule, as amended); DEC-11, DEC-13, DEC-22 (amended), DEC-23.

**Goal:** `just setup` becomes a cold start over the CLI: it keeps an
existing settings file, imports an existing `.env`, or writes a fresh one
with `init` after asking whether to use NordVPN, and no longer writes
`.env`. README.md and the planning docs describe Stage 1 as implemented, and
the maintainer's own installation migrates in place and passes `dlx doctor`
with the VPN on. This is the last delivery item: once it is accepted,
`/relay-project` closes P0001.
